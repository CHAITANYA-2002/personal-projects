media that remain the property of their respective owners. This file records where every

If you fork, publish or deploy this repository, review this file first and confirm that your

## 3. Driver photographs — `assets/drivers/*.jpg`

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Source** | English Wikipedia article lead images, retrieved through the Wikipedia REST API |
| **Licence** | Individual images carry their own licences — most are Creative Commons (commonly CC BY-SA), some are public domain, and a minority are non-free images hosted under Wikipedia's fair-use policy |
| **Count** | ~329 portraits, prioritising race winners and the current grid |
| **Used for** | Podium cards, the driver dossier, and championship leader cards |

**Attribution:** each photograph's author and licence are recorded on the corresponding
Wikipedia article. Because licences differ per image, verify the individual image page before
reusing any single photograph outside this project.

**No synthetic faces.** Where no photograph is available the interface falls back to a
team-coloured monogram (for example `JF`). The application never renders an AI-generated or
stand-in face in place of a real person.

## 4. Constructor logos — `assets/teams/*.png`

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Source** | English Wikipedia constructor article images |
| **Status** | **Registered trademarks of their respective owners** — Scuderia Ferrari, McLaren, Mercedes-AMG Petronas, Oracle Red Bull Racing, Atlassian Williams Racing, and others |
| **Count** | 18 team marks |
| **Used for** | Identifying the team a result belongs to in tables, podium cards and register rows |

These marks are used **nominatively** — that is, purely to identify the team whose data is
being displayed, in the same way a sports results site or an encyclopaedia entry does. They
are not used as branding for this project, do not imply any endorsement, affiliation or
sponsorship, and are not modified beyond scaling.

Teams without an available mark fall back to a neutral team-coloured monogram rather than a
substitute or invented logo.

> If this project is ever used commercially, remove `assets/teams/` and rely on the monogram
> fallback, which is generated by the application itself.

## 7. Analysis authorship

The database design, the SQL in `01_schema.sql` through `10_capstone_thesis.sql`, the two
notebooks, the Streamlit application and the written findings are the work of this project's
author, produced as coursework.

**Interpretation notice:** the findings describe statistical associations in a historical
dataset. They do not establish causation, and definitions such as "mechanical DNF" are
documented judgement calls rather than official FIA classifications. See the limitations
section of [`README.md`](README.md).
